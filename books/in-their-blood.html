In Their Blood

The Espositos claimed another victim. This time, they may not get away with it.

A peace summit. A blizzard. And somehow, the Espositos have already produced a corpse.

The family has come to Niagara Falls to end a war that has been tearing them apart for two years. But only a few hours after arriving, someone is dead.

Then the roads close. The power begins to fail. And no one is leaving.

As two detectives try to make sense of a family where everyone has a motive and no two stories quite agree, old crimes resurface and carefully buried secrets begin to unravel.

Thirty miles away, Linda wants nothing to do with any of it.

But the Espositos have a way of pulling people back into their orbit. And what begins as one more family disaster will lead Linda toward questions about her own past, and a history far older than the body waiting in the hotel.

In Their Blood is the third novel in the In Their Place series: a story of murder, inheritance, and the dangerous difference between the history we are given and the truth we inherit.
